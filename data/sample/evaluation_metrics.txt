The MTRAGEval framework evaluates multi-turn conversational RAG along two
axes: retrieval quality and end-to-end generation quality.

Retrieval is scored using nDCG@k and Recall@k over the ranked list of
documents returned by the system. These metrics reward both relevance and
position - moving a relevant document up the ranked list increases nDCG
even when Recall stays constant.

End-to-end generation is scored using a harmonic mean over three signals:
RB_agg measures document-level grounding (did the answer rely on the
retrieved evidence?), RL_F measures faithfulness (does the answer assert
things the evidence supports?), and RB_llm is an LLM-as-judge answer
quality score.

On its final submission, H-RAG achieved nDCG@5 = 0.4271 on Task A and a
harmonic mean of 0.3241 on Task C, with component scores RB_agg = 0.2488,
RL_F = 0.2703, and RB_llm = 0.6508. The relatively high RB_llm score
indicates fluent answers, while the lower grounding scores point to
evidence selection - not fluency - as the dominant bottleneck.
